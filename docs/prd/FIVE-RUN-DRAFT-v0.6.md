# Five Run — featured draft v0.6

This amendment changes discovery while preserving NBA-only eligibility, 500 records, G/G/F/F/C, six offers and the approved attribute/overall rules. Rules version 4; catalog version remains 3.

Every round selects one uniformly random eligible name from a curated recognition pool, then five uniformly random other eligible players from the full position pool. These five may naturally include additional featured names. All six positions are shuffled. Previously seen players stay excluded, the offers are saved before reveal, and all 500 players remain eligible. The 12 G / 12 F / 6 C featured pool cannot be exhausted by valid earlier rounds.

The editorial pool is independent of overall, attributes and simulation strength. It is not a scientific popularity ranking or a rarity system. Its criterion is recognizable names across generations: established NBA icons and familiar modern stars. No athlete receives an attribute bonus and no opponent adapts to the user's choices.

- G: Michael Jordan, Kobe Bryant, Magic Johnson, Stephen Curry, Oscar Robertson, Jerry West, Dwyane Wade, Allen Iverson, James Harden, Steve Nash, Jason Kidd, John Stockton.
- F: LeBron James, Larry Bird, Kevin Durant, Giannis Antetokounmpo, Tim Duncan, Dirk Nowitzki, Charles Barkley, Scottie Pippen, Kawhi Leonard, Julius Erving, Karl Malone, Kevin Garnett.
- C: Kareem Abdul-Jabbar, Wilt Chamberlain, Bill Russell, Shaquille O'Neal, Hakeem Olajuwon, Nikola Jokić.

This deliberately improves the chance to choose a fantasy roster. It does not claim to solve competitive balance: with automatic maximum-OVR choices, titles increased from 68.55% to 79.00% in 2,000 fixed-seed tournaments per scenario. Preferring featured names reached 74.45%; random choice reached 30.80%. Observed player sessions must guide any subsequent difficulty change; historical opponent snapshots stay fixed.

The official 7a0 research and controlled comparisons are in `docs/draft-research.md`; no code or data from 7a0 was copied. Following the explicit logo correction, historical teams reference current official NBA franchise logos from the NBA CDN. The roster year is not the logo vintage; no logo binaries are redistributed. See `docs/team-logos.md`.
