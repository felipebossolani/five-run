# Five Run — NBA-only scope v0.5

This amendment supersedes the eligibility and opponent scope in the original v0 PRD and expansion v0.4. Explicit direction from Felipe: focus only on NBA and male players. Keep the original documents as the decision history.

- The final catalog contains 500 distinct men who actually played in the NBA, with 200 G / 200 F / 100 C. Being drafted or signing without an NBA appearance does not qualify. International male players with NBA experience remain eligible.
- Production records explicitly declare `gender: 'male'` and `nbaExperience: true`. Women and players without NBA experience are excluded from the active catalog.
- Opponents are historical NBA franchises with a defined year and five coherent roster members. Keep the 12 researched NBA identities; remove national teams and clubs outside the NBA. Each run selects seven distinct opponent identities.
- Keep the six-option draft, compact comparable attributes, concise style tags and sourced career highlights/achievements approved in v0.4. Full style descriptions remain in player details.
- Keep year-specific opponent attribute snapshots and the corrected contribution of shooting to medium-distance two-point accuracy. Overall weights remain unchanged; do not inflate Jordan to a target overall.
- Rules/catalog version 3 invalidates incompatible prior runs with an explanation while preserving settings. Insecure HTTP URLs, deterministic saves and browser checks remain required.
- Editorial and artistic review remain human release gates. Researching the remaining eligible records is implementation work and must not be presented as complete until all 500 records and portraits exist.
