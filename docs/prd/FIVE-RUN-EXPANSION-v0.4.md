# Five Run — expansion v0.4

This amendment preserves `FIVE-RUN-PRD-v0.md` as the original baseline. Requested by Felipe following Gabriel's feedback.

- Expand the catalog to 500 unique athletes: 200 guards, 200 forwards, 100 centers. Maintain six editorial attributes and one draft card per athlete.
- Offer six distinct eligible draft options per round, including a mobile 3×2 layout. Retain explicit inspection/confirmation, no rerolls, and no repeated offers within a run.
- Add a concise playing-style line to cards. Details show selected peak years, up to three notable team stints with years, and up to three sourced achievements. These are career highlights, not an exhaustive biography.
- Replace fictitious opponents with real historical NBA teams, national teams and leading clubs from other countries. Each identity has a defined year and a coherent five-player roster from that period. Artwork remains original and does not reproduce official logos or uniforms.
- Historical opponents use explicit era-specific attribute snapshots when their roster differs from draft peak windows. Do not imply that an athlete's peak ratings describe every season.
- Audit overall calibration across roles and the Michael Jordan anchor. Preserve approved positional weights unless Felipe approves a change. Do not inflate ratings to force an arbitrary overall target.
- Bump incompatible catalog/rule versions, explain invalidation and preserve settings. Maintain insecure HTTP support, deterministic outcomes, save/reload, accessibility, both locales and the browser test gates.

Human editorial and artistic approval remains required; source facts and estimates must be distinguished.
