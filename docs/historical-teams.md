# Historical teams and season snapshots

Reviewed 2026-11-02. The active pool contains 12 historical NBA champions. The year labels the championship season. Five players are selected from the corresponding roster in G/G/F/F/C game slots; they are not a claim that the five started every game. Team colors are original UI choices; no team logos or uniforms are included.

Sources document team rosters and season context. Six-attribute season snapshots are subjective editorial estimates, not statistics reported by those sources. Each has a year and a role-specific note. Ratings and cross-era comparisons remain subject to editorial review; snapshots do not overwrite draft ratings.
