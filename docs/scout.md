# Five Run scouting — catalog v3

The catalog contains 500 male players who appeared in the NBA: 200 guards, 200 forwards and 100 centers. The group is a game roster category and can differ from a player's other professional positions. Historical team rosters use these catalog IDs and categories.

Each entry includes an individual career profile and citations for its selected career highlights and achievements. Sources include NBA player and team profiles, NBA season and award records, the Naismith Memorial Basketball Hall of Fame, and other identified basketball archives. A season year means the calendar year in which the NBA season ended. A cited stint may cover only a notable season rather than a complete tenure. Some distinctions provide career context outside the selected window; their own years are shown explicitly.

Peak windows are editorial three-season comparison periods, grounded in career context and published honors or season records. They are not a claim that every season was a career best. Shooting, finishing, playmaking, defense, rebounding and athleticism are subjective game ratings on a 40–99 scale, comparing players' roles within their selected periods. No source independently certifies the ratings; cross-era comparisons and individual styles necessarily involve judgment.

Source review was performed during catalog construction, but the entire 500-player dataset has not received exhaustive independent fact checking or human editorial approval. Confidence is recorded per player; low confidence should be treated as a review priority.
